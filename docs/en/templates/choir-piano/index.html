Template guide

Blank staff paper for SATB choir and piano

Print blank staves for four voices and piano, with room for lyrics and closer piano staves. A free, customizable A4 template.

Customize and print

The vocal staves have extra space for lyrics, while the piano stays grouped more closely. Edit each block to suit your arrangement.

A4 · 12 staves · Staff line spacing: 2 mm

SATB
4 staves · Between staves (mm): 12
Piano
2 staves · Between staves (mm): 6

Only blank staves, without clefs, notes or watermarks. Block names help you configure the sheet and are not printed.

Prepare your sheet

Open the template in the generator, adjust line weight, spacing or paper size, and choose Print. You can also save it as a PDF from the browser’s print dialog.

A small space for music

Pentagrama started with a simple need: flexible manuscript paper, quick to print and free of advertising on the sheet. Made for writing music freely.

This project is free and follows Value for Value: if it helps you and you want to support it, you can send a voluntary microdonation over Lightning. Recommending it helps too.
