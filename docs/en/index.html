A small space for music

Pentagrama started with a simple need: flexible manuscript paper, quick to print and free of advertising on the sheet. Made for writing music freely.

This project is free and follows Value for Value: if it helps you and you want to support it, you can send a voluntary microdonation over Lightning. Recommending it helps too.

Improved keyboard navigation, focus and screen reader descriptions, with the current interface preserved.

Footer text formatting: size, typeface, style and alignment. Clearer, more compact icons for image placement.

One backup to export and restore templates, image footers, the current sheet and preferences. File validation and a summary before replacing data.

2.5.0

October 22, 2026

Custom footers with an image, text or both. Adjustable size and layout, color and transparency, and a library of reusable footers saved in the browser.

2.4.1

October 22, 2026

Consistent thumbnails and direct access to the editor from the catalogue. Name and date above the title and instructions, including in the sidebar.

2.4.0

October 22, 2026

Title and instructions with size, three typefaces, bold, italic, underline and alignment in a compact panel. Browser preferences and formatting preserved in templates, links and print.

2.3.5

October 21, 2026

More useful statistics on suggested and personal templates and the options in printed or saved sheets, without sending text or layout codes.

2.3.4

October 21, 2026

New writing-space worksheet, beginner sheets with name and date, and larger orchestral staves. Updated catalogue with translated previews and pages matching the editor.

2.3.3

October 20, 2026

Unclipped focus highlights on segmented controls. Custom footer text shares one row with the address and reference, while title and instruction alignment use one three-state button each.

2.3.2

October 20, 2026

Writing space replaces Notes area. Consistent typography, colors, heights and alignment throughout the sidebar, plus a grouping selector styled like the other dropdowns.

2.3.1

October 20, 2026

The notes reserve hides staves that no longer fit, preserving their original spacing. The gap follows the smaller vertical margin, with a visible count and better-spaced controls.

2.3.0

October 20, 2026

Hybrid sheets with staves and a blank, ruled, grid or dotted notes area. Cleaner headers with independent alignment; shorter references exclude personal text, which stays in saved templates and shared links.

2.2.0

October 20, 2026

Headers with a title, instructions and name/date fields in the selected language. A custom text footer and front-only header option, all preserved in saved templates and shared links.

2.1.2

October 20, 2026

Book view on tablets too, keeping the front fully visible with overflow on the left. Odd/even labels, a subtle binding shadow and more consistent, compact double-sided and top-space controls.

2.1.1

October 20, 2026

A compact double-sided control and automatic inside/outside margins. Book-style preview with the front on the right and back on the left, plus the author’s photo in V4V.

2.1.0

October 20, 2026

Two sides with an adaptive preview and two-page printing. Optional front-only top space and inside/outside binding margins.
